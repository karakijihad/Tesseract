TESSERACT is free software under the **GNU Affero General Public License,
v3**. The full text is [in the repository](https://github.com/karakijihad/Tesseract/blob/main/LICENSE);
this page is the short version of what it means for you.

## What you can do

- **Read all of it.** Every line that runs on your machine is published. For
  something with this much access to your computer, that seems like the least
  it can offer.
- **Run it, for anything.** There is no personal-use clause and no seat count.
- **Change it, and share what you changed.** Fork it, strip out what you do not
  want, wire it to a model this repository has never heard of.

## What the licence asks back

Only one thing, and it is the difference between the AGPL and the ordinary GPL:
**if you run a modified version as a service other people use over a network,
those people are entitled to your source.** Running your own modified copy on
your own machine, privately, obliges you to nothing.

That clause is the reason for this licence rather than a permissive one. A
local-first assistant that someone else can quietly turn into a closed hosted
product, with your data going somewhere you cannot see, is the failure mode
worth writing into the terms.
